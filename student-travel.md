Student travel award applications for the WSSCI Spring 2026 Technical Meeting at the Oregon State University Portland Center are now open. The award (in the past ~$150 per student, assuming that sufficient funds are available) applies to students who are presenting at the meeting, must travel greater than 50 miles, and are current members of the Western States Section of the Combustion Institute. Please note: preference may be given to students with oral presentations and financial need, pending total student requests. The deadline for applications is 3/20/26.

To apply, please complete this form (<https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScrcRbTzp2LzNz6K2-v4rm3njixvurf6ib-_AfIDGZ7YgE8sg/viewform?usp=publish-editor&pli=1&authuser=0>) AND attach a PDF statement from your advisor verifying the below four points by 3/20/26. For any questions, please email [Sara McAllister](mailto:sara.mcallister@usda.gov) and please include "[Spring 2026 WSSCI Student Travel Award]" in the email subject line.

Eligibility requirements:
 1. Membership in the Western States Section
 2. Presentation of a paper
 3. Confirmed adviser attendance
 4. Registration as a student at your home institution
 5. Travel greater than 50 miles (as measured from the student's home institution to the event)
